# 🍃 Food Rescue AI
### Don't Waste It. Donate It. Make Someone Smile.

**Food Rescue AI** is an AI-powered social impact platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting food donors, NGOs, orphanages, shelters, and volunteers.

The platform helps redistribute surplus food and essential items to people who need them through intelligent matching, donation management, and AI-assisted coordination.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Large amounts of usable food and essential resources are wasted every day, while many people and organisations struggle to access basic necessities.

Food Rescue AI aims to bridge this gap by making donations easier, smarter, and more accessible.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Donor Dashboard:** Upload and manage surplus food and essential items.
- **NGO Dashboard:** Discover available donations and request resources.
- **Volunteer Management:** Coordinate pickups and deliveries.
- **AI-Powered Matching:** Suggest suitable NGOs based on donation details and location.
- **Smart Donation Categorisation:** Use AI to understand donation descriptions and images.
- **Food Safety Checks:** Apply predefined eligibility rules with human review.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Verify organisations and manage platform activities.
- **Impact Tracking:** Monitor completed donations and resources redistributed.

## 👥 User Roles

2. NGOs / Receivers
3. Volunteers
4. Administrators

## 🤖 AI Innovation

Food Rescue AI aims to implement five specialised AI agents:

1. Donation Intake Agent
2. Safety & Rules Agent
3. NGO Matching Agent
4. Logistics Agent
5. Verification & Impact Agent

These agents will work together to improve donation discovery, coordination, and transparency.

AI recommendations will assist users, while humans retain responsibility for food-safety approval and final decisions.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Donor registers and uploads a donation.
2. AI analyses donation information.
3. Suitable verified NGOs are recommended.
4. NGO requests the donation.
5. Donor and receiver coordinate collection.
6. Receiver confirms successful delivery.
7. The platform updates its impact records.

## 🌍 Our Mission

To reduce avoidable waste, support communities, and ensure that surplus resources reach people who need them.
